Privacy policy

Updated November 9, 2026

Deal Notes is a static website. We do not run user accounts or store personal data on our own servers. This page explains the third-party services that may set cookies or collect data when you visit.

Does this site use cookies?

Yes, through third-party services: Google Tag Manager and analytics, and, if enabled, Google advertising. Amazon may also set cookies after you click an affiliate link.

Cookies are small files stored by your browser. You can block or delete them in your browser settings; the site still works without them. The site also stores your light or dark theme choice in your browser.

What analytics do you use?

We use Google Tag Manager, which can load Google Analytics, to understand which pages are read and how people find the site.

This data is aggregated and is not used to identify you. Google's use of this data is covered by Google's privacy policy. You can opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

Do you show Google ads?

Not currently. If we enable Google AdSense, Google and its partners may use cookies to show ads based on your visits to this and other sites.

You can manage ad personalization at Google Ad Settings and learn more at how Google uses cookies in advertising.

How do affiliate links affect my privacy?

When you click a "Check price on Amazon" link, Amazon records that you came from this site so it can credit a commission. Amazon's handling of your data is covered by its own privacy notice.

We do not receive your name, address or order details from Amazon.

What happens if I contact you?

We use the details you send only to reply. Contact form submissions are handled by HubSpot when the form is enabled.

Do you collect data from children?

No. This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Will this policy change?

If our services change, we will update this page and the date at the top.
