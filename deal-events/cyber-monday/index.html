Amazon Cyber Monday 2026: date and shopping tips

When is Cyber Monday 2026?

Cyber Monday is Monday, November 30, 2026, the Monday after Thanksgiving weekend. Amazon's Cyber Monday guide, published November 4, 2026, confirms the date.

Cyber Monday started as the online counterpart to in-store Black Friday. At Amazon the two now run together as one long holiday sales period.

Is Cyber Monday different from Black Friday at Amazon?

Mostly in timing. Amazon treats Black Friday and Cyber Monday as one holiday shopping stretch, and many deals carry across both.

As of November 9, 2026, Amazon has not posted official start and end dates for a 2026 Black Friday Week and Cyber Monday event. See our Black Friday 2026 page for what is confirmed so far.

Should I wait for Cyber Monday or buy on Black Friday?

If the price on Black Friday matches the item's recent low, buy it then. Waiting risks the item selling out for only a small chance of a lower price.

Popular items, especially Amazon's own devices and toys, can run out of stock. A price tracker or Amazon's own price history view helps you decide whether a price is already at its floor. See our price history guide.

What is the return window for Cyber Monday purchases?

Amazon's return policy page says most items bought from November 1 to December 31, 2026 can be returned through January 31, 2027, and Apple-brand products by January 15, 2027.

Check each product page for category exceptions before you buy.
