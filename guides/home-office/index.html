Frequently asked questions

What is the most worthwhile home office upgrade?

For most people it is the item you use most: a comfortable keyboard and mouse for writers and analysts, or a good webcam for people on video calls all day.

Do I need a 4K webcam for work calls?

Usually not. Most video call apps send far less than 4K, so good lighting and a solid 1080p camera matter more.
