Frequently asked questions

When is outdoor gear cheapest?

Camping gear is often discounted at the end of summer and during Prime Day. Coolers and chairs also show up in Black Friday sales. Check price history before treating any sale as a good deal.

Do I need an expensive cooler?

Only if you need ice to last several days. For day trips, a basic cooler works. Premium coolers earn their price on multi-day trips.

What should every day-hike kit include?

Water, a headlamp, a map or downloaded route, layers, snacks, sun protection and a small first-aid kit.
