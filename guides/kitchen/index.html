Frequently asked questions

What kitchen tools should I buy first?

A chef's knife, a large skillet, a cutting board, an instant-read thermometer and a digital scale cover most daily cooking. Add specialty gadgets only after you know you will use them.

Are kitchen items cheaper on Prime Day or Black Friday?

Popular kitchen brands are often discounted during both events, but not every listed discount is real. Check the item's price history before buying, as explained in our price history guide.

Is it safe to buy knives and cookware from third-party sellers on Amazon?

Check the seller name under the buy button. Items sold by the brand or by Amazon itself are the lowest-risk option for avoiding counterfeits.
