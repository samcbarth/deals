Frequently asked questions

What tools should a first-time homeowner buy?

A cordless drill with two batteries, a bit set, a tape measure, a multi-bit screwdriver, a hammer, a level and a utility knife cover most first-year projects.

Is a 12V drill strong enough?

For furniture, shelves, curtain rods and light repairs, yes. Frequent drilling into masonry or large lumber calls for an 18V or 20V drill.

How do I know if my ladder or tool was recalled?

Search the brand and model at cpsc.gov/Recalls. In November 2026, Little Giant recalled about 3.66 million ladders with Rapid Locks, covered in our news section.
