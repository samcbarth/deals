What is Amazon's holiday return window for 2026?

Amazon's return policy page says most items bought between November 1 and December 31, 2026 can be returned through January 31, 2027. Apple-brand products bought in that window have a shorter deadline of January 15, 2027.

Amazon says Black Friday 2026 is November 27 and Cyber Monday is November 30, and it has early deals live in its Holiday Shop. If an item you want is already at a price you are happy with, buying early avoids stock-outs and the extended return window still covers you.
