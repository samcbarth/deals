Frequently asked questions

Are cheap USB chargers on Amazon safe?

Some are, many are unknown. Stick with established brands, look for safety certification marks such as UL on the product or listing, and avoid chargers that run very hot.

What size power bank do I need?

A 10,000mAh bank recharges most phones once or more. Bigger banks are heavier, and airlines limit large batteries in carry-on bags, so check before you fly.

Do I need a separate cable for my laptop?

Possibly. Laptops often need higher wattage than phones, so match both the charger and the cable to the wattage your laptop's maker lists.
