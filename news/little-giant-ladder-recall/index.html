Little Giant recalls about 3.66 million ladders with Rapid Locks

November 5, 2026 · By Sam Barth

RecallsTools

The CPSC says Little Giant ladders with Rapid Locks can suddenly retract if the locks are not fully engaged, posing a fall hazard. Owners should stop using them and get a repair kit.

Little Giant has recalled about 3,662,000 ladders with Rapid Locks, according to a U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission notice dated November 5, 2026. The CPSC says the ladders can suddenly retract if the Rapid Locks are not fully engaged, posing a fall hazard.

The CPSC tells owners to stop using the recalled ladders until they review the information at littlegiantladders.com/pages/recalls, or through the QR code on the ladder, and contact Little Giant for a repair kit.

Why it matters: ladders are a common tool purchase, including online. If you own a Little Giant ladder, check it against the recall before your next project.
