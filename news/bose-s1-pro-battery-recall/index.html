Bose recalls S1 Pro and S1 Pro+ speaker battery packs over fire risk

November 5, 2026 · By Sam Barth

RecallsConsumer tech

About 634,759 lithium-ion battery packs for Bose S1 Pro and S1 Pro+ portable Bluetooth speakers can overheat and ignite, the CPSC says. Bose is offering free replacement packs.

Bose has recalled about 634,759 lithium-ion battery packs used in its S1 Pro and S1 Pro+ portable Bluetooth speaker systems, according to a CPSC notice dated November 5, 2026. The CPSC says the packs can overheat and ignite.

Owners should remove the recalled battery pack and stop using it. The speakers can still be used while plugged into AC power. Bose asks owners to register the pack's serial number at bose.com/support/s1-battery-recall for a free replacement.

The CPSC also warns not to put the recalled battery in the trash, curbside recycling or store battery drop boxes, because recalled lithium-ion batteries need special disposal.

Why it matters: battery recalls are a reminder to buy chargers and power banks from established brands and to check recall notices when a device runs hot.
