Apple TV 4K (4th generation) goes on sale Nov. 13 at $299

October 13, 2026 · By Sam Barth

Consumer techLaunches

Apple's new Apple TV 4K adds an A19 chip, far-field microphones for hands-free Siri and four-speaker HomePod surround support. Pre-orders opened Oct. 13; stores get it Nov. 13.

Apple announced the fourth-generation Apple TV 4K on October 13, 2026, with pre-orders starting that day and availability in stores beginning Friday, November 13. Apple lists the U.S. price at $299.

According to Apple, the A19 chip delivers up to 50 percent faster CPU performance and up to 2x faster GPU performance than the previous model, and adds a dedicated AV1 decoder. Three built-in far-field microphones let owners talk to Siri hands-free, and the box can drive a four-speaker surround setup with HomePod mini 2 or HomePod (2nd generation) speakers.

Why it matters: a new model often pushes retailers to discount the outgoing one. If you are shopping streaming boxes for the holidays, compare the older model's price history with the new one's features.
