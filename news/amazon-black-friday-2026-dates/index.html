Amazon confirms Black Friday on Nov. 27 and Cyber Monday on Nov. 30

October 29, 2026 · By Sam Barth

Retail eventsAmazon

Amazon's holiday guide confirms the 2026 Black Friday and Cyber Monday dates and says early deals are already live in its Holiday Shop. It has not yet posted Black Friday Week event dates.

Amazon published its 2026 Black Friday guide on October 29, confirming that Black Friday falls on Friday, November 27, and Cyber Monday on Monday, November 30. The company says shoppers can browse early deals in its Holiday Shop now.

What Amazon has not done yet is publish start and end dates for a 2026 Black Friday Week event. Deal trackers have floated predicted dates, but we are not repeating guesses.

Why it matters: early deals are useful for gifts that tend to sell out, and Amazon's extended holiday return window covers most purchases made from November 1. Check each item's price history before treating an early deal as the best price.
