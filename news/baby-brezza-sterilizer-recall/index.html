Baby Brezza recalls SuperFast sterilizer dryers over overheating

November 5, 2026 · By Sam Barth

RecallsBaby

About 29,000 Baby Brezza SuperFast sterilizer dryers can overheat while plugged in and running, posing a burn hazard, the CPSC says. Baby Brezza is offering free replacements.

Baby Brezza Enterprises has recalled about 29,000 SuperFast sterilizer dryers in the U.S., according to a CPSC notice dated November 5, 2026. The CPSC says the units can overheat when plugged in and operating, posing a burn risk if the surface is handled.

Owners should stop using the recalled sterilizer dryers and contact Baby Brezza for a free replacement, including free shipping.

Why it matters: baby gear is a common gift and registry purchase. Anyone who received or bought this model should check the recall notice.
