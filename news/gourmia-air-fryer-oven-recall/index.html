Gourmia recalls about 175,951 9-slice digital air fryer ovens

November 5, 2026 · By Sam Barth

RecallsKitchen

The doors on Gourmia 9-slice digital air fryer ovens can close unexpectedly when bumped, posing a burn hazard, the CPSC says. Gourmia is mailing free repair kits.

Gourmia has recalled about 175,951 9-slice digital air fryer ovens, according to a CPSC notice dated November 5, 2026. The CPSC says the oven doors can unexpectedly close when bumped, posing a burn hazard to people reaching in for hot food.

Owners should stop using the ovens and contact Gourmia for a free repair kit by mail. The kit adds a doorstop and installation instructions. Gourmia's recall page is gourmia.com/recall.asp.

Why it matters: countertop ovens and air fryers are popular holiday deal items. If you already own this model, fix it before the busy cooking season.
